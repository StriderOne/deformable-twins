## Inference
Correlated VI has slightly worse calibration error and training objective, but
slightly better continuation error in this single run. Object marginal widths
remain close to prior and are slightly larger on average. This does not establish
a reproducible advantage or convergence. Seeds share their nominal value, but
factor initialization and additional draws change sample streams. The reference
was trained through checkpoint continuations; low-rank training started from CMA.
Marginal widths alone do not reveal learned correlations or test spatial locality.

farthest midpoint-distance quartile 0.0003105, all other object edges 0.0003934.

Inference: neighbors have larger average correlation for this reference, but
all correlations are weak; this does not support strong neighboring dependence.
One reference, one seed and rank-limited VI cannot establish physical independence
or refute the spatial hypothesis globally. Full dense covariance was not allocated.

Under P-0005, sampled object reference IDs uniformly before measuring
correlations; exact audit settings are in the artifact plan.json. Physical
Pearson correlations were computed by bivariate quadrature; convergence error
between quadrature orders was below 4.04e-5. Neighbor edges share a vertex;

Neighbor mean absolute correlation exceeds far mean for 236/512 references
(46.09%). Across references, neighbor mean is 0.003415 versus far 0.000515;
median paired difference is -0.00000515. The average advantage is driven by
some references, not a majority-wide effect.
Selected example 50154: neighbor mean 0.11699 versus sampled far mean 0.01588;
maximum neighbor correlation magnitude 0.3641, sampled far maximum 0.2640.
Example 89019 has a neighbor magnitude 0.5337. These examples were selected
AFTER inspecting differences and must not be treated as representative evidence.

Inference: there are localized stronger dependencies in this learned posterior,
but neither universal neighbor dependence nor distant independence is supported.
Only one fitted rank-limited posterior is examined; edge pairs are dependent,
so no independent-sample significance claim is made.
